Levies and money

What Are Strata Levies?

2 February 20265 min readBy Strata United

If you own, manage, or invest in a strata-titled property, chances are you’ve encountered strata levies, sometimes regularly, sometimes unexpectedly. These contributions are a standard part of strata living across Sydney and nearby areas, including Parramatta. Yet many owners still ask the same question: what are strata levies, and where does the money actually go?

This guide explains how strata levies work, how they’re calculated, and why they matter for the long-term health of a building.

What Are Strata Levies?

In simple terms, what are strata levies? They are mandatory financial contributions paid by lot owners to cover the shared costs of running and maintaining a strata scheme. Every owner pays a portion based on their unit entitlement, which is usually linked to the size and value of their lot.

The strata levy meaning is straightforward. Levies exist to make sure common property is maintained, bills are paid on time, and future works are properly funded. Without them, a building would struggle to function.

In New South Wales, strata levies are set through an approved budget at the annual general meeting (AGM). Once approved, they become payable by all owners in the scheme.

Strata Levies: Meaning in Practical Terms

The strata levies' meaning becomes clearer when you look at what they support day to day. Levies are not arbitrary fees. They are calculated based on actual and projected expenses for the building.

This includes everything from routine cleaning to long-term capital planning. When levies are set properly, owners avoid sudden financial shocks, and buildings remain compliant with safety and regulatory requirements.

In Sydney’s high-density developments, especially around Parramatta, accurate levy planning is essential due to building scale, shared facilities, and compliance obligations.

The Two Main Types of Strata Levies

Most strata schemes collect two separate levies. Each serves a different purpose.

Administrative Fund Levies

Administrative fund levies cover everyday operating costs. These are the expenses required to keep the building running smoothly.

Common inclusions are:

  • Building insurance
  • Cleaning and gardening
  • Electricity for common areas
  • Lift servicing
  • Fire safety checks
  • Strata management fees

These levies tend to be paid quarterly and can change year to year depending on service costs and usage.

Capital Works Fund Levies

Capital works fund levies are set aside for major repairs and future projects. Think of them as long-term planning funds.

They usually cover:

  • Roof replacement
  • External painting
  • Structural repairs
  • Lift upgrades
  • Waterproofing works

Older buildings or those with shared amenities often require higher capital works contributions. A strong capital works fund reduces the risk of special levies later on.

How Are Strata Levies Calculated?

Strata levies are calculated using a budget approved by the owners. The strata manager prepares a detailed forecast based on past spending, upcoming works, and compliance needs.

Each owner’s share of the strata levy is based on unit entitlements, which are registered with the strata plan. Larger apartments or commercial lots generally pay higher levies than smaller residential units.

Professional Strata Management Services play a key role here. Accurate budgeting ensures levies remain fair, transparent, and aligned with actual building needs.

What Do Strata Levies Typically Cover?

Many owners assume levies only pay for cleaning and insurance. In reality, strata levies fund a wide range of essential services.

These commonly include:

  • Building and common property insurance
  • Repairs and ongoing maintenance
  • Compliance with NSW safety standards
  • Record keeping and financial reporting
  • Contractor management
  • Emergency repairs

For repair-related expenses, owners often interact with Repairs & Maintenance processes managed by the strata manager.

Why Strata Levies Vary Between Buildings

No two strata schemes are the same. Levy amounts vary widely across Sydney and Parramatta due to several factors.

These include:

  • Age of the building
  • Number of lots
  • Shared facilities like lifts, pools, or gyms
  • Insurance premiums
  • Past maintenance history

A newer building with minimal shared facilities may have lower levies initially. An older building may require higher contributions to address wear and future repairs.

Good levy planning avoids deferred maintenance, which often leads to higher costs down the line.

Strata Levies and Property Value

Well-managed strata levies often support stronger property values. Buyers look closely at levy history, capital works funds, and financial records before committing.

Buildings with underfunded levies can raise red flags. They suggest future special levies or unresolved maintenance issues.

Access to organised records through Strata Forms and clear financial reporting gives buyers confidence and supports smoother transactions.

What Happens If Strata Levies Aren’t Paid?

Strata levies are legally enforceable. When levies go unpaid, the owners' corporation can apply interest, issue notices, and recover debts through formal processes.

Consistent non-payment can affect cash flow, delay repairs, and create tension within the building. That’s why proactive communication and professional management matter.

If you’re unsure about levy obligations or payment schedules, contact us so we can help clarify next steps.

Reviewing Your Current Strata Levy Structure

Owners sometimes assume their levies are fixed forever. That’s rarely the case. Levies should be reviewed regularly to reflect real costs.

Signs it may be time to reassess include:

  • Repeated special levies
  • Deferred maintenance
  • Rising insurance costs
  • New compliance requirements

A review may also prompt owners to consider changing providers.

Why Professional Strata Management Matters

Managing strata levies requires experience, local knowledge, and financial oversight. Poor planning can lead to disputes, unexpected expenses, and declining building conditions.

A trusted provider like Strata United supports owners with structured budgets, transparent reporting, and clear advice. This approach helps buildings across Sydney and Parramatta remain compliant, well-maintained, and financially stable.

Final Thoughts on Strata Levies

So, what are strata levies really about? They are the backbone of a functional strata scheme. When set correctly, they protect the building, support long-term planning, and reduce stress for owners.

Clear budgets, fair contributions, and professional oversight make a noticeable difference. Whether you’re reviewing your current levies or joining a new scheme, informed decisions today can prevent costly issues tomorrow.

For tailored guidance based on your building’s needs, speaking with experienced strata professionals can provide clarity and confidence moving forward.
