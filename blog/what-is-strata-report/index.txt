24:T188d,<p>If you’re dealing with a strata property in Sydney or around Parramatta, chances are someone has told you to “get a strata report.” That advice is solid. A strata report often reveals more about a building than any brochure or sales pitch ever will.</p>
<p>For business owners, investors, developers, and committee members, this document helps answer one simple question: What are we really getting into?</p>
<p>Below is a clear, practical look at what a strata report is, what it covers, and why it plays such a big role in NSW property decisions.</p>
<h2>What Is a Strata Report?</h2>
<p>So, what is a strata report in real terms?</p>
<p>It’s a written summary of how a strata scheme operates behind the scenes. The report is created after reviewing the owners&#39; corporation’s official records. These records show how money is handled, how decisions are made, and how the building has been looked after over time.</p>
<p>If you’re wondering what is a strata report in NSW is, it’s a standard due diligence document used across New South Wales before buying or committing to a strata property. It helps buyers and stakeholders avoid surprises that only show up after settlement.</p>
<h2>What Does a Strata Report Actually Show?</h2>
<p>A good strata report doesn’t just list documents. It tells a story about how the building is run.</p>
<h3>Financial position</h3>
<p>This section outlines levies, budgets, arrears, and balances in both the administrative and capital works funds. It gives a clear sense of whether the scheme is financially steady or regularly stretched.</p>
<h3>Meeting history</h3>
<p>Minutes from meetings show what issues keep coming up and how they’re handled. You can often tell whether a committee is proactive or constantly putting out fires.</p>
<h3>Maintenance and repairs</h3>
<p>Past and planned works appear here, offering insight into the building’s condition. This information often overlaps with details found in a strata inspection report, especially where recurring defects or major works are involved.</p>
<h3>Insurance</h3>
<p>The report confirms building insurance, coverage levels, and recent claims. This matters more than many buyers expect, especially in mixed-use or commercial buildings.</p>
<h3>Rules and compliance</h3>
<p>By-laws shape how properties can be used, leased, or altered. For B2B buyers, these details can directly affect operational plans.</p>
<h2>Why Strata Reports Matter for Business Buyers</h2>
<p>For B2B buyers, strata reports are less about curiosity and more about risk.</p>
<p>They often reveal:</p>
<ul>
<li>Levy arrears that affect cash flow</li>
<li>Capital works funds that don’t match the building’s age</li>
<li>Ongoing disputes within the owners&#39; corporation</li>
<li>Maintenance issues that may lead to special levies later</li>
</ul>
<p>This is why experienced buyers treat strata reports as essential, not optional. Strong strata management also plays a role here. Buildings supported by professional <a href="/strata-management-services">strata management services</a> tend to produce clearer, more reliable records.</p>
<h2>Strata Report vs Strata Inspection Report</h2>
<p>These terms are often confused.</p>
<p>A strata inspection report is based on reviewing documents held by the owners&#39; corporation. It does not involve inspecting the physical building.</p>
<p>A building inspection is separate and focuses on physical condition. Many buyers commission both to gain a complete view.</p>
<p>Together, they help answer two different questions:</p>
<ul>
<li>How has the scheme been managed?</li>
<li>What condition is the building in today?</li>
</ul>
<h2>Strata Report Cost in NSW</h2>
<p>Another common question is the strata report cost.</p>
<p>In Sydney and nearby areas, prices vary depending on the size of the scheme, the volume of records, and turnaround time. Costs usually sit in the hundreds, sometimes higher for complex buildings.</p>
<p>While it’s a small upfront expense, the report often highlights issues that influence negotiation, planning, or even whether to proceed at all.</p>
<h2>Who Can Request a Strata Report?</h2>
<p>Access to strata records in NSW follows set rules. Buyers, owners, and authorised parties can request access through the owners&#39; corporation or strata manager.</p>
