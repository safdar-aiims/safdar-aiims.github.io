<h2>Understanding Strata Management in Melbourne</h2>
<p>Strata management comes to the foreground in strata title properties, a popular type of property development these days, especially in high-rise, multi-unit developments. With common property areas to maintain and shared facilities provided, strata management plays a crucial role in managing these areas to maintain a standard of living for the community. Strata management, when done skillfully, helps to foster a sense of community among the residents. Not only that—rules are established, too, to promote harmonious living so that members of the community, owners, and tenants alike, have a guide to behaviour that most closely supports the community. Otherwise, there would be disagreements among people of diverse living habits.</p>
<p>Strata management is done primarily via a body corporate—a strata company or an owner&#39;s corporation—that has the primary responsibility to manage the strata property. While management falls quite largely onto the strata company, the management involved is also one in which the many strata lot owners are also directly involved. The lot owners participate as a collective, representing the community as a whole. Therefore, the management process can be a bit complicated. Many development corporations turn to outsource professional help from strata consultancy almost immediately so that they can also focus on their construction projects under way.</p>
