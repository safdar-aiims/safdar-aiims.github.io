08 / Considering switching?

It may be time to change

If your strata company is not delivering on your expectations, changing is easier than most committees expect. It does need to be done in the correct order, with all the boxes ticked, to stay compliant and to avoid expensive mistakes. Here is the whole process, with nothing left out.

01 / The process

The seven steps, in order

  1. 01

    Line up your replacement

    Before you let your current manager go, make sure you have a better replacement waiting. Shortlist a few agencies, ask each for a quote and a full schedule of services, and choose on substance rather than on the lowest line.

  2. 02

    Check your agreement

    Obtain a copy of the management agreement from your current manager to determine the expiry date and the termination period required. This one step sets your timeline.

  3. 03

    Contact the owners

    Get the strata roll, also from your manager, and let each owner know an extraordinary general meeting is being called.

  4. 04

    Issue the agenda

    Provide a meeting agenda to all owners with the date, time and venue of the EGM, giving at least 14 days notice, along with a motion to confirm the minutes of the previous meeting, a motion to terminate the current agreement and a motion to appoint the new strata manager.

  5. 05

    Hold the EGM

    The meeting can be held at the complex for convenience. There is no obligation to inform or invite the outgoing manager.

  6. 06

    Pass the motion

    The motion needs to be passed by a majority of owners, in person or by proxy.

  7. 07

    We take it from here

    Once the vote is passed and the minutes are recorded, we handle the rest: informing the previous manager of the termination and arranging collection of the records after the notice period.
