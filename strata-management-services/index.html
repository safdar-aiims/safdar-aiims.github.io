03 / Common questions

Cover, fees and response times

Read all the FAQs

A strata manager runs the day to day administration of your scheme on behalf of the owners corporation. That covers the money, levy notices, budgets, trust accounts and reporting, the meetings and minutes, the records and the strata roll, insurance and claims, contractors and maintenance, and compliance with the Strata Schemes Management Act 2015.

The decisions stay with the owners corporation. Carrying them out, and keeping the evidence that they were carried out, is the manager's job.

Anything outside the schedule of services, and every one of those items is listed in the summary fee table of our proposal before you appoint us. Some are charged at cost, such as courier charges, the annual fire safety statement, defects administration and attending the building outside office hours. Others are paid by the individual lot owner concerned rather than the owners corporation, such as a notice of overdue levy or instructions to recover outstanding levies. Nothing is held back for an invoice later.

As a flat annual management fee for each scheme, based on the building and what it needs. We set it out in a written proposal with a full schedule of services and a summary fee table, so your committee can see exactly what the fee covers and what is charged outside it. There are no hidden charges. Send us the building address and the number of lots, and we will come back within one business day with a proposal.
