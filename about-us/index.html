About Strata United

The people behind the buildings

Strata United manages residential and commercial schemes across the Sydney metropolitan area. We are a tight team of property professionals with over 20 years of combined experience, and we take on the optimum amount of work so that every building we manage thrives under proper supervision.

01 / What we are all about

We find solutions to achieve results, rather than telling clients what they cannot do

Strata United is passionate about giving owners corporations and their members a strata management service that is industry leading and competitively priced. We empower our clients by making sure they have the information they need to make decisions about their scheme and to understand its administration.

Do you know how the important issues affecting your strata scheme are handled? Our clients do.

How we hold ourselves to account

  • Honesty

    We tell you what we know, including when the news is not good.

  • Transparency

    Every figure is in your portal. You should never have to ask us for it.

  • Responsiveness

    Correspondence answered within 24 hours. Emergencies answered at any hour.

  • Diligence

    The unglamorous work, done on time: certificates, renewals, arrears, minutes.

  • Quality

    Premium service without the premium being the only thing you notice.

  • Accountability

    If something is ours to fix, we say so and we fix it.

02 / How we work

End to end, personalised, systemised

  • End to end

    Management and supervision of every building we take on, from the levy notice to the fire safety statement.

  • Personalised

    Premium and friendly service without compromising quality. There is no cookie cutter approach here.

  • Systemised

    Sophisticated electronic communication and record management, so information is found in seconds rather than days.

03 / Leadership

Elias Aktila, Chief Executive Officer

Elias is responsible for running all aspects of Strata United. He brings more than 20 years of executive experience driving growth in technology businesses, and has spent his time here applying that thinking to the day to day reality of strata living.

Before Strata United, Elias held senior sales and marketing roles across the strata and property sector, served on the board of a business chamber of commerce, and was recognised by peers as one of the state’s leading strata executives. He holds a DBM from the Excelsior College of Sydney, School of Business.

04 / Where we work

Sydney metropolitan, with local knowledge that matters

We manage schemes from Parramatta and Westmead through Liverpool and Warwick Farm, across to Homebush, Silverwater, Bankstown and Mascot, and into the inner city. Different suburbs bring different buildings, different by-law pressures and different contractor markets, and our managers work to that reality rather than a single template.
