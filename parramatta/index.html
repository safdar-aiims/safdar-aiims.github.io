01 / Strata in Parramatta

Three kinds of building in the same few postcodes

Parramatta is Sydney’s second CBD and it builds like it. High density residential towers, mixed use podiums with retail below and apartments above, and older walk up blocks on the surrounding streets, all in the same few postcodes. Those are three different management problems. Towers bring lift contracts, fire systems and building managers. Mixed use schemes bring strata management statements and cost allocation arguments. Walk ups bring ageing common property and capital works decisions that owners have been deferring. We manage all three, and we price each on what it actually needs.
