Request a quote

Get a quote for your scheme

Tell us the building address and roughly how many lots, and we will come back within one business day with a flat fee proposal and the full schedule of services that fee covers.

  • A real proposal, not a number

    You get the same document our clients get: what is covered, what is not, and what it costs.

  • No obligation and no pressure

    Plenty of committees quote us to benchmark what they pay now. That is a perfectly good reason to call.

  • We will tell you if you should stay

    If your current agreement has a long notice period or your scheme is mid claim, we will say so.

  • One business day

    That is the same response standard our clients get, so you can judge us on it before you appoint us.

What happens next

  1. We confirm receipt and ask anything we need to price properly.
  2. We send a written proposal with a full schedule of services and a fee table.
  3. If your committee wants it, we present at your meeting.
