06 / How we do it

Technology that brings people together

Strata United leads the way in applying the technology available to this industry. These are the systems you get access to as a client, and they exist for one reason: so that transparency is something you can check rather than something we claim.

Request a quote
  • 01

    Strata Vault

    Secure information management

    The only independent information management platform designed specifically for your strata scheme. Information is uploaded once and shared securely with authorised service providers, when and where they need it.

    • Transparency and productivity for owners corporations
    • Fast file retrieval and collation
    • Authorised access for service providers
  • 02

    StrataVote

    Online voting and meetings

    Lot owners vote online from any device. Votes are tallied and carried into the meeting record, so by the time the meeting ends your minutes are pre-compiled and ready to send.

    • Create meetings and issue agendas
    • Pre-voting, auto poll vote and autofill documents
    • Electronic voting and attendance register
    • Runs AGMs, EGMs and committee meetings from one place
  • 03

    The Strata United app

    Your scheme in your pocket

    We built an app so your strata is not something you can only deal with at a desk. Manage your strata needs in one application, at your fingertips.

    • One application
    • Any device
    • Always current
