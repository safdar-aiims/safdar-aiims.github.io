01 / Owner access

What the portal lets you do

  • View the contact details recorded for your properties
  • View the agent and tenant details recorded for your properties
  • Contact the agency to update your details
  • View your levies paid to date
  • View general financial details for your properties
  • View lot entitlements for your properties
  • Retrieve your username and change your password

02 / Why it exists

Reliable strata management, visible from your side

Daily automated banking, reconciliations and reporting kept current mean that what you see in the portal is what is actually in the account. Owners should not need to lodge a request to find out where their levies went.

03 / Access

Getting set up

  1. Ask your strata manager for portal access, or send us your name, building address and lot number.
  2. We verify you against the strata roll.
  3. You receive a username and set your own password.

Cannot find what you need?

Your manager can help, and so can the office. We answer correspondence within one business day.
