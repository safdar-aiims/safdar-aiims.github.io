Where we work

Local strata management across Sydney

Our office is in Ultimo and our managers work across the Sydney metropolitan area. Different suburbs bring different buildings, different by-law pressures and different contractor markets, so our managers work to the building in front of them rather than a single template.

Areas we serve

  • Parramatta

    Towers, mixed use podiums and older walk ups across Sydney’s second CBD.

    Parramatta CBD · Harris Park · Granville · Merrylands · Rosehill · North Parramatta · Westmead

    Strata management in Parramatta

  • Liverpool

    Newer apartments in their defect period and established townhouse complexes.

    Liverpool · Warwick Farm · Casula · Moorebank · Lurnea · Prestons · Chipping Norton

    Strata management in Liverpool

  • Westmead

    Investor owned buildings around the health and education precinct.

    Westmead · North Parramatta · Wentworthville · Toongabbie · Pendle Hill · Mays Hill

    Strata management in Westmead

  • Carlingford

    Villa, townhouse and low rise schemes, and the newer apartment buildings among them.

    Carlingford · Telopea · Dundas · Dundas Valley · Epping · Beecroft · Oatlands

    Strata management in Carlingford
