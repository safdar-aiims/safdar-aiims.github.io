04 / Commercial strata

Commercial strata management

Strata United understands the importance of a well administered building and its direct impact on employees, tenants and new business opportunities.

Request a quote

01 / Why it matters

First impressions last, and they start in the lobby

With Strata United as your commercial strata manager, your building’s image and environment are kept immaculate and managed by experienced people. Common areas, shared services, contractors, compliance and the money behind all of it are handled so your tenants notice the building working rather than the building failing.

02 / Included

What commercial schemes get

  • Common property and shared services coordinated
  • Contractor engagement, WHS documentation and site access managed
  • Fire safety statements and lift certificates lodged on time
  • Insurance valuations and claims handled end to end
  • Budgets and levies built around planned capital works
  • Financial reporting suited to owners who report to a board
  • By-law and strata management statement review for mixed use schemes
  • 24/7 emergency response

Mixed use schemes

Retail below, residential above, one building, two very different sets of expectations. Mixed use schemes need a strata management statement that is actually applied, and cost allocation that both sides accept. We review the statement, apply it consistently, and put the numbers where everyone can see them.
