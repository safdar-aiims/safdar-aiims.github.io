Repairs and maintenance

Repairs and maintenance that get closed out

The complaint we hear most about other managers is not that a repair was expensive. It is that nobody said what was happening. Maintenance here is tracked from the first report to the final invoice, and the committee can see where it is at any point.

Submit a request1300 445 900

01 / The process

How a repair runs

  1. 01

    Reported

    An owner, a tenant, a contractor or a site visit raises the issue. Everything is logged against the building, not against a memory.

  2. 02

    Assessed

    We determine whether it is common property or lot owner responsibility under the Strata Schemes Management Act 2015, and tell you which it is.

  3. 03

    Quoted

    Quotes are arranged from contractors we have used before, with scope written so quotes are comparable.

  4. 04

    Approved and engaged

    Within delegated limits we proceed. Above them it goes to the committee with a recommendation, not just a pile of PDFs.

  5. 05

    Completed and closed

    Works are checked, invoices are paid from the correct fund, and the job is closed in the record where it can be found again in three years.
